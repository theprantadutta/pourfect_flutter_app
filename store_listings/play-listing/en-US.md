## Full description

A relaxing ball sort puzzle: pour the balls between tubes until each one holds a single color. Calm color sorting you can play anywhere, even offline.

That's the whole game. No lives, no waiting to play again, and a gentle clock you're free to ignore.

NO WIFI? NO PROBLEM.

All 150 levels are already on your phone, so Pourfect plays the same on a plane, on the subway, or with one bar of signal. Zen mode plays offline too. Only the Daily Pour, weekly events and leaderboards need a connection.

EVERY LEVEL CAN BE SOLVED

This is the part most sorting games get wrong. Every one of our 150 levels was checked by a solver before it shipped, and we know the fewest moves each one takes. You will never hit a level that simply cannot be finished.

A RELAXED CLOCK, NOT A COUNTDOWN

Each board quietly shows your time next to a generous par time. Finish fast and you earn more points. Take it slow and you simply earn fewer: your stars come from your moves alone, and nothing ever runs out. The clock pauses whenever you leave the app.

ZEN MODE: NO CLOCK, NO STARS

When you just want to pour, Zen gives you endless boards with nothing at stake. Pick Calm (4 colors), Steady (6) or Deep (8). There's no clock, no stars and no leaderboard, only a count of the boards you've poured. Every Zen board is made on your phone and checked by the same solver. Zen opens once you've cleared level 30.

BUILT TO BE RELAXING

- Unlimited undo, always free. Change your mind as often as you like.
- No lives and no energy. Play as long as you want.
- A hint when you want one, and an extra tube if you're stuck.
- Soft sounds, gentle music and haptics, each with its own switch in Settings.

A LITTLE SOMETHING EVERY DAY

- The Daily Pour: one new board a day for everyone, with a streak and a calendar to keep it going.
- Weekly events: seven boards each week, with points for pace and a leaderboard to climb.
- The Collection: earn ball skins and tube themes from star chests and streaks.

PLAYS NICELY WITH YOUR MUSIC

Pourfect mixes with whatever you are already listening to. It will never pause your podcast or your playlist to play a sound effect.

DESIGNED TO BE SEEN CLEARLY

Every ball carries a distinct shape as well as a color, so the game stays playable if you are color blind. It isn't a mode you have to find in a menu: it's how the game is drawn, and every skin keeps the shapes. There's a bold-symbols setting if you want them larger still.

150 LEVELS THAT ACTUALLY BUILD

- First Pours: three and four colors, gentle enough to learn on.
- Finding Rhythm: five to seven colors, where it starts to click.
- Deep Water: eight to ten colors, real puzzles now.
- Mastery: ten colors, down to a single spare tube. Room for nothing sloppy.

The difficulty was tuned level by level, with easier levels deliberately placed along the way so it never becomes a grind.

QUIET AND RESPECTFUL

No account required. No banner ads, and no ad after every level. Nothing here is trying to trick you into a purchase.

Pour, sort, relax.
