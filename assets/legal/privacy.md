## Introduction

Pourfect ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy
explains what information the Pourfect mobile application (the "App") collects,
why, and what control you have over it.

**Pourfect is playable entirely offline.** You can install the App and finish
the whole campaign without an account, without a network connection, and
without any of the data below leaving your device. Everything described here is
either optional, or is the minimum needed to run a feature you chose to use.

By downloading and using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## What We Collect

### 1. Account information

Pourfect creates an **anonymous account** for you through Firebase
Authentication the first time online features are used. This is a random
identifier. It is not linked to your name, your email address, or any Google or
Apple account unless you choose to sign in.

- **Anonymous user ID** — a random Firebase identifier
- **Authentication tokens** — short-lived, used to prove the App is talking to
  us on your behalf

**Important, because it works differently to what people expect:** an anonymous
account does not survive uninstalling the App or moving to a new device. If you
uninstall Pourfect, that account and its cloud copy of your progress become
unreachable. We say this plainly rather than describing anonymous sync as
"backup," because it is not one.

If you later sign in with Google or Apple, we additionally receive the email
address, display name, and profile picture associated with that account, as
provided by the sign-in provider.

### 2. Game progress

Only when online features are used:

- Level number, stars earned, and your best move count per level
- When you first completed each level
- Total stars, levels completed, and highest level reached
- Daily challenge results: moves used, stars, and how long the attempt took
- Daily challenge streak

### 3. Display name (optional, and the leaderboard opt-in)

Leaderboards show only players who have **chosen a display name**. Until you set
one, you do not appear on any leaderboard and no name of yours is shown to
anyone. We never generate a public name for you.

Once set, your display name and your score are visible to other players on the
leaderboards you appear on.

### 4. Device and technical information

- Platform (Android or iOS) and app version
- Country or region, derived on our servers from your connection — **not** GPS
  and not a precise location
- Your device's UTC time offset, used only so a reminder notification arrives in
  the evening where you are rather than in the middle of your night
- A push notification token, if you allow notifications

### 5. Purchase information

If you buy Remove Ads:

- The purchase token and order ID issued by the app store
- Whether the purchase verified successfully, and the reason if it did not
- The date of purchase

**We never receive or store your card number, bank details, or billing
address.** Payment is handled entirely by the app store.

### 6. Analytics

We use **Firebase Analytics** to understand where players get stuck. The events
we record are:

`level_start`, `level_complete`, `level_abandon`, `hint_used`, `undo_used`,
`power_up_used`, `ad_shown`, `ad_completed`, `iap_viewed`, `iap_purchased`

These carry the level number, move counts, star ratings, durations, and whether
a hint or ad was involved. They are about the puzzle, not about you.

### 7. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through **Google AdMob**:

- **Interstitial ads** — occasionally between levels, never during play and
  never when you restart a level
- **Rewarded video** — only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a hint

**Pourfect does not show banner ads.**

Google AdMob and its partners may collect an advertising ID, device
identifiers, and approximate usage data to serve and measure ads. See
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### 8. What we do NOT collect

We want this list to be as useful as the one above:

- No precise location or GPS coordinates
- No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No payment card details
- No friends lists, messaging, or social graph — the App has no social features
- No advertising profile built by us; we do not sell or share your data with
  data brokers

## How We Use It

- **To run the game** — save your progress, score your levels, serve the daily
  challenge, and rank leaderboards
- **To keep scoring honest** — every score is recalculated on our servers
  against the puzzle's proven optimal solution, so an impossible score is
  rejected
- **To verify purchases** — confirm with the app store that a Remove Ads
  purchase is genuine, and make sure one purchase unlocks one account
- **To send the notifications you allowed** — at most one daily challenge
  reminder per day, in your local evening, and only if you have played a daily
  challenge before
- **To improve the game** — find the levels where players give up, and re-tune
  them
- **To show ads** in the free version, subject to your consent choices

## Advertising Consent

Where consent is required — including the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland — the
App presents Google's UMP consent form **before requesting any ad**. If consent
is unavailable or you decline, the App does not request ads at all.

You can change your choice at any time from the App's Settings, where a privacy
options entry appears if your region requires one. You can also reset your
advertising ID in your device settings.

## Where It Is Stored, and For How Long

Progress and account data are stored on our own servers and in Google's
infrastructure, and are encrypted in transit using TLS.

- **While your account exists:** we keep your progress so you can come back to
  it
- **When you delete your account:** your progress, statistics, daily challenge
  results, purchase records, and push tokens are removed from our servers
  immediately, and your sign-in identity is deleted
- **Backups:** residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days
- **Local data:** progress saved on your device is removed when you uninstall
  the App

## Your Rights and Controls

**Delete your account and data.** The App offers account deletion in Settings.
It removes everything described above from our servers. If you can no longer
open the App, email us and we will process the same deletion.

**Play without an account.** The campaign works offline with no account at all.

**Stay off the leaderboards.** Simply do not set a display name.

**Turn off notifications** in your device settings, or decline them when asked.

**Change your ad consent** from Settings where your region offers it.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct,
export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it
or withdraw consent. This includes rights under the **GDPR** (EU/UK) and the
**CCPA/CPRA** (California). We do not sell personal information, and we will
not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights. To make a
request, email us using the address below.
