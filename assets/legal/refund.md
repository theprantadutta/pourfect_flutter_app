## The short version

Pourfect sells **one thing**: a one-time **Remove Ads** purchase. It is not a
subscription, it does not renew, and you will never be billed for it twice.

Because the app store processes the payment, **the store issues refunds, not
us.** The fastest route is always your store's own refund flow, and the links
are below. If the store cannot help you, email us anyway — we would rather sort
it out than leave you with something you did not want.

## What you can buy

| Item | Type | Renews? |
|---|---|---|
| Remove Ads | One-time, non-consumable | No — never |

There are no subscriptions, no coin packs, no loot boxes, and no consumable
purchases in Pourfect.

Hints are earned free or by watching an optional rewarded video. **Hints are
never sold for money**, so there is nothing to refund there.

## How to request a refund

### Google Play (Android)

1. **Within 48 hours of purchase**, you can usually refund it yourself:
   open the Play Store → your profile → **Payments & subscriptions** →
   **Budget & order history** → select the order → **Request a refund**.
2. **After 48 hours**, use Google's refund request form at
   https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 and Google will review
   it.

### Apple App Store (iOS)

Apple handles all refunds for purchases made on iOS. Go to
https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose the purchase, and select
**Request a refund**. You can also use Settings → your name → **Media &
Purchases** → **View Account** → **Purchase History**.

### If the store declines, or something went wrong on our side

Email **prantadutta1997@gmail.com** with:

- the store you bought from (Google Play or App Store),
- the **order ID** from your receipt, and
- what went wrong.

We will look into it and respond. Where the fault is ours — you were charged
and the entitlement never arrived, or it was removed in error — we will fix the
entitlement directly and support your refund request with the store.

## When we will actively help

We think these cases deserve more than a link:

- **You paid and ads did not stop.** Try **Settings → Restore purchases**
  first, which re-checks your purchase with the store. If that does not fix it,
  email us — this is a bug on our side and we will correct it.
- **You were charged twice for Remove Ads.** It is a one-time purchase, so a
  second charge is an error. Contact the store for the refund and tell us so we
  can investigate.
- **Remove Ads stopped working after a reinstall.** Use **Restore purchases**.
  Note that one purchase unlocks **one account at a time**: restoring it onto a
  new account moves it there and removes it from the old one. That is what
  lets you keep what you bought after reinstalling.
- **The purchase never completed but money left your account.** Pending
  payments — carrier billing or parental approval, for example — can take time
  to settle. If it has settled and you still have nothing, email us with the
  order ID.

## What we cannot do

Being straight about the limits:

- **We cannot process the refund ourselves.** We never receive your payment;
  the store does. Only the store can return the money.
- **We cannot refund a purchase made on another store account** than the one
  contacting us.
- **We cannot refund time spent playing**, ads watched, or hints used. Rewarded
  video costs attention rather than money, so there is nothing to return.
- **We cannot restore progress from an uninstalled anonymous account.** An
  anonymous account cannot survive uninstalling the App. Signing in with Google
  or Apple is what makes progress portable, and we say so in the App rather
  than after the fact.

## Timeframes

- We aim to reply to refund emails within **5 business days**.
- Store refund decisions and processing times are the store's own; Google and
  Apple typically complete an approved refund within a few business days,
  though your bank may take longer to show it.

law where you live. If your local law gives you a stronger right to a refund
than this policy describes, **that law applies**, not this policy.
