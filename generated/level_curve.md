# Pourfect level curve

Level set version 1, 150 levels.

`difficulty` is the calibrated 0-100 score. `forced` is the
fraction of states on the optimal path with only one meaningful
move; `branch` is the mean number of distinct options. Breather
levels are marked and must sit at least 25% below the running
average of the five levels before them.
