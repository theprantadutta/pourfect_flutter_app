Privacy Policy

For Pourfect: Ball Sort Puzzle · Last updated 28 August 2026

The short version. Pourfect does not ask who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, and no name, email address or phone number is ever collected. The game is fully playable offline, and your puzzle progress is stored only on your own device.

What we do collect is anonymous information about how the game is played, so we can find levels that are too hard or broken, plus the standard identifiers used to show ads.

Who we are

Pourfect is developed and published by Pranta Dutta. If you have any question about this policy or about your data, write to privacy@pranta.dev and we will reply.

What we collect

WhatWhyWho processes it
Gameplay events To see which levels players finish, retry or give up on, so we can fix levels that are unfair or unclear. Google Firebase Analytics
Device and app information Collected automatically alongside those events: device model, operating system version, country, language, and the app version. Google Firebase Analytics
Advertising ID To show ads and limit how often the same ad is repeated. This is a resettable identifier provided by your device, not a name. Google AdMob
Purchase confirmation If you buy “Remove Ads”, Google tells the app that the purchase succeeded so the ads can be switched off. Google Play Billing

The gameplay events we record are: starting a level, finishing a level, leaving a level unfinished, using a hint, using undo, using a power-up, and seeing or completing an ad. Each carries the level number and figures such as the number of moves made and how long the level took.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • Your precise location. Analytics infers only an approximate country from your IP address.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. The app never requests these permissions.
  • Any account, because the game does not have accounts.

Your puzzle progress

Which levels you have solved, your stars and your best move counts are saved on your device only. They are not uploaded to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. You can erase them at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

Advertising

Pourfect shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to make ads more relevant and to limit repetition. You can reset or delete that identifier at any time in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones.

Google's own handling of that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Buying Remove Ads stops the interstitial ads that appear between levels. Rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch one in exchange for a hint or a power-up.

Children

Pourfect is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Analytics events are retained by Firebase for 14 months, then deleted automatically. Advertising identifiers are held by Google under their own retention rules, linked above. We keep no separate copy of either, and because nothing we collect identifies you, we cannot look up “your” data.

Where it is processed

Firebase Analytics and AdMob are operated by Google, and data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google's transfer safeguards are described in their Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's privacy settings, at any time.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in the same place. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Erase your progress from Settings inside the app.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app, which deletes all locally stored data.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have rights of access, correction, erasure and objection under the GDPR. Because we hold no information that identifies you, we usually cannot connect a request to a particular person — but write to us and we will help as far as we are able.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions, corrections or complaints: privacy@pranta.dev.
